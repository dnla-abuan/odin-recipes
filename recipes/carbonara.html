Creamy Carbonara

A rich and creamy pasta dish perfect for any occasion.

About This Recipe

Carbonara is a popular pasta dish made with spaghetti, creamy sauce, cheese, and bacon. This homemade version is simple to prepare and perfect for family meals.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time 15 minutes
Cooking Time 20 minutes
Servings 4 people

Ingredients

  • 250 grams spaghetti
  • 200 ml all-purpose cream
  • 1/2 cup grated cheese
  • 150 grams bacon, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Chopped parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Boil water in a pot and cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  2. Drain the pasta and set aside.
  3. In a pan, cook the chopped bacon until lightly crispy.
  4. Add butter, garlic, and onion, then cook until fragrant.
  5. Pour in the all-purpose cream and stir gently.
  6. Add the grated cheese and season with salt and pepper.
  7. Add the cooked spaghetti and mix until evenly coated.
  8. Garnish with parsley and extra cheese before serving.
