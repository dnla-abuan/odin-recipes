Chocolate Chip Cookies

Sweet, soft, and delicious homemade cookies.

About This Recipe

Chocolate chip cookies are a popular sweet treat made with flour, butter, sugar, and chocolate chips. They are perfect for snacks and desserts.

Recipe Information

Preparation Time 15 minutes
Cooking Time 12 minutes
Servings 12 cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mix the butter, brown sugar, and white sugar in a bowl.
  3. Add the egg and vanilla extract.
  4. Add the flour and baking soda, then mix well.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Place spoonfuls of dough onto a baking tray.
  7. Bake for 10–12 minutes or until golden brown.
  8. Allow the cookies to cool before serving.
